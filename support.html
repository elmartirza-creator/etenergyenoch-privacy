Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The app works offline. All 108 chapters are included and available without a connection.

Is my data sent to the cloud?

No. Your notes, reflections, highlights, bookmarks, reading history and progress are stored only on your device. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I start a reading plan?

Open the Study tab, choose Plans, and tap Start Plan. Each day lists its chapters. Tap a chapter to read it, and mark the day complete when you have finished.

What are the reflection questions?

Each day of the five section reading plans has one open question for your own thinking. Open the day in Plans to write a reflection. Your saved answers appear in Study > Reflect, where you can edit or delete them.

How do I track my progress?

The Study tab's Journey view shows how many chapters you have opened, your progress through each plan, and a Continue button.

How do I save or share my notes?

Use the share icon at the top of the Study tab to export your notes and reflections as a text file. The file opens in the iOS share sheet, so you choose where it goes.

How do I highlight text?

Tap a verse number and choose Highlight to highlight the whole verse. To highlight words or sentences, select them and choose Highlight. To remove a highlight, select it again and choose Remove Highlight.

Can I compare the Ethiopic and Greek readings?

Yes. Chapter XXII has two source readings. Use the control at the top of the chapter to show both, the Ethiopic (E) reading, or the Greek (Gᵍ) reading. No text is removed.

What do the brackets and symbols mean?

Tap Symbols in this text at the top of a chapter. The key follows R. H. Charles's own key to his 1917 translation.

What translation is used?

The text is the 1917 translation of the Book of Enoch by R. H. Charles, with the reading groupings and reflection questions added by ETenergy. The groupings and questions are not part of the original text.

Is my reading data synced to the cloud?

No. Syncing across devices is not offered.

How do I change the text size or appearance?

Open Settings. You can change the text size and choose a light or dark theme.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details of what the app stores and what leaves your device.
