Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the app require an internet connection?

A: No. The app works completely offline. All 108 chapters are included in the app and available without any internet connection.

Q: Is my reading data synced to the cloud?

A: No. Your bookmarks, reading history, and preferences are stored only on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

Q: Can I adjust the text size and appearance?

A: Yes. The app includes customizable font sizes (8pt to 28pt), light and dark themes, and adjustable line spacing so you can read comfortably.

Q: How can I save my favorite chapters?

A: Use the bookmark feature (tap the ribbon icon at the top of a chapter) to save chapters you want to return to. Your bookmarks are saved locally on your device.

Q: Where can I find privacy information?

A: See our Privacy Policy for complete details about how we handle your data.

Q: What translation is used in the app?

A: The app uses the R.H. Charles 1917 English translation of the Book of Enoch, widely regarded as one of the most accurate and scholarly renderings.

Q: How many chapters are included?

A: All 108 chapters of the complete Book of Enoch are included in the app.
