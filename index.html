Privacy Policy

Enoch — The Book of Watchers

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Enoch — The Book of Watchers ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Key Point: This app works entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App operates completely offline. We do not:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, phone, location)
  • Collect usage data or analytics
  • Require user accounts or login credentials
  • Transmit data to any server or cloud service
  • Use tracking cookies or advertising identifiers
  • Request permission to access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
  • Store anything outside your device

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your reading preferences and history are stored only on your device and never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your current reading position (which chapter you last read)
  • Bookmarked chapters
  • Reading history (chapters you've recently opened)
  • Display settings (font size, light/dark theme preference)

All this data remains on your device. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it. We have no access to it.

4. iOS System Permissions

The App requires no special permissions from iOS. It does not request:

  • Access to your calendar, contacts, or photos
  • Access to your camera or microphone
  • Location services
  • Health data
  • Bluetooth or Wi-Fi information
  • Keyboard tracking or clipboard access

5. Internet & Network

The App does not:

  • Require an internet connection to function
  • Check for updates from our servers (you update via App Store)
  • Connect to any backend service, database, or API
  • Send crash reports or diagnostics (this is handled by Apple's App Store Connect, not by us)

Everything works offline after download.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not use:

  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Third-party payment processors
  • Cloud storage services
  • Content delivery networks (except Apple's for app distribution)

7. App Store & Apple

Apple collects standard App Store analytics and crash data through App Store Connect. This is separate from the App itself and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not access or use this data.

8. Advertising

The App contains no advertisements. There are no ad networks, sponsored content, or promotional material.

9. In-App Purchases

The App has no in-app purchases. It is free and contains all 108 chapters without any paywalls, trials, or upsells.

10. Children's Privacy

This App does not target children under 13. However, it is safe for all ages because:

  • No personal data is collected from anyone
  • No tracking occurs
  • No account creation is required
  • No contact with external services happens

If you have concerns, contact the developer directly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App means you accept any changes.

13. Summary

What we collect: Nothing.

What we store: Your local reading data (on your device only).

What we transmit: Nothing.

What you control: Everything (delete the app to delete all data).

You own your data. We don't access it.
