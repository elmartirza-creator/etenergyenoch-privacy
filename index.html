Privacy Policy — ETenergy Enoch: Study

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ETenergy Enoch: Study ("the App") is a study and reference app for the Book of Enoch. This policy explains what the App stores, where it is stored, and what leaves your device.

Summary

  • The App works offline. It does not need an account or an internet connection.
  • The App does not send your personal information, notes, reflections, highlights or reading progress to us or to anyone else.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and uses no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.
  • Everything you write or mark is stored in the App on your device. Deleting the App removes its local data; separate device backups may retain copies.

1. Information the App stores on your device

The App saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • Notes: the title, the note text, any passage you chose, and the verse reference (for example "Verse 2 (G^g)").
  • Reflections: your answers to the study questions in the reading plans, and the question text as it was when you saved the answer.
  • Highlights: whole verses you highlight, and words or sentences you select and highlight.
  • Bookmarks: the chapters you bookmark.
  • Reading position and history: the chapter you were last reading, and a list of up to 50 recently opened chapters.
  • Journey progress: which chapters you have opened (so the App can show a count), and your progress through each reading plan, including the days you mark complete.
  • Display settings: text size and theme (light or dark).
  • Study groupings: the App's built-in groupings of chapters, and any groupings the App has saved.
  • Protected copies of stored data that the App cannot read. These are kept on your device so nothing is lost, and they are not sent anywhere.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, health data, or device identifiers. We do not collect usage analytics or crash reports through the App.

3. Network use

The App does not connect to the internet to work. It does not send the information described in section 1 to us or to any third party.

4. Exporting and sharing your work

You can export your notes and reflections as a text file. The App creates the file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet, so you choose where it goes (for example Files, Mail or Messages). The App does not send the file anywhere by itself. Once you share the file, its handling is governed by the app or service you shared it with.

5. Backups

If you back up your iPhone or iPad (for example to iCloud or to a computer), the backup may include the App's stored information. We do not have access to your backups.

6. Permissions

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or any other iOS permission.

7. Children

The App does not ask for your age and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Third parties

The App uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or payment services. Apple's App Store may collect and handle information according to Apple's privacy policy.

9. Text and attribution

The Book of Enoch text is the 1917 translation by R. H. Charles. The groupings of chapters and the reflection questions in the App are editorial material by ETenergy and are not part of the original text.

10. Deleting your information

Delete the App to remove its stored information from your device. If you use iPhone or iPad backups, copies of this information may remain in those backups until removed according to the backup service or device settings. Export your notes and reflections first if you want to keep them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same address.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact elmartirza@gmail.com.
